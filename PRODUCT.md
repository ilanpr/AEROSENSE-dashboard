# AEROSENSE

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Public visitors and residents view environmental conditions. Industrial teams and governance cadres manage assigned monitoring locations. Administrators assign access per location; ordinary accounts cannot grant themselves management access.

## Product Purpose
Connect local air sensor readings, regional environmental context, historical records and an AI assistant so people can understand conditions and follow up with local managers.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The public landing page explains the system and links to public monitoring and sign-in. Authorized location operators/admins configure operational warning, danger, recovery and notification thresholds. CSV exports support spreadsheet analysis. Sensor readings and regional model/API estimates remain distinct. Optical dust is not validated PM2.5 without calibration; eCO2 is not direct CO2. Configurable thresholds are operational settings, not a declaration of regulatory compliance. Missing or stale data must not imply clean air.

## Brand Commitments
Use the supplied blue AEROSENSE logo, supplied Falcon mascot, existing sensor illustration, Inter, blue/cyan gradients and gentle decorative motion. Preserve the established dashboard identity and mobile readability.

## Approved Landing Purpose
The user confirmed a public introduction for general visitors and area managers: system overview, main features, workflow, “Lihat pemantauan” and “Masuk” calls to action. Management access is assigned by an administrator per location.

## Evidence on Hand
The repository contains the product UI, public and authenticated APIs, role-scoped memberships, sensor rules, and supplied brand illustrations. Local demo data is explicitly synthetic; it is not physical measurement evidence. No customer logos, testimonials, performance statistics or compliance certification are supplied.
